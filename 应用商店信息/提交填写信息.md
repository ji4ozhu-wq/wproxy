## Description
WProxy is a fast, private proxy and VPN client for iOS. Add your own servers or subscription link, tap Connect, and your traffic is routed through the node you chose — with an interface that feels right at home on iPhone, iPad, and the new foldable iPhone Duo.

BUILT FOR YOUR OWN SERVERS
Connect VMess, VLESS, Trojan, and Shadowsocks nodes. Add them manually, scan a QR code, paste a share link, or import a subscription that updates itself.

SMART ROUTING
Keep your local network and any regions you choose direct, and send everything else through your node. Rule-based and global modes are one tap apart.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts, no analytics, no ads. Your nodes and settings are stored only on your device, and WProxy does not log your traffic or destinations.

MADE FOR EVERY SCREEN
Native SwiftUI design that adapts from iPhone SE to iPad and iPhone Duo. Light theme, system typography, and SF Symbols throughout.

Note: WProxy is a client. It does not provide servers or subscriptions; you need your own node or subscription to connect.

## App Review Notes（Guideline 5.4 要求）

- **VPN usage:** WProxy uses the NetworkExtension Packet Tunnel Provider (`NEPacketTunnelProvider`) to route the user's traffic through the proxy server the user configures. The app contains an embedded, statically linked Xray-core; no executable code is downloaded at runtime.
- **User-provided servers:** WProxy is a client only. It ships with no servers and no accounts. To test, add a test node via Add Node → Paste from Clipboard, or scan the sample QR code included in the Review Notes attachment. (测试节点待提供)
- **Data declaration:** The first launch shows a data statement + consent screen (no accounts, no analytics, no ads; nodes and settings stored locally; traffic only to the user's server).
- **No logging:** The app does not log or record traffic, destination addresses, or connection history.
- **Third-party data:** Country-routing data comes from the public v2fly/geoip release (MaxMind GeoLite2, CC BY-SA 4.0) and is refreshed over HTTPS; no user data is uploaded. While the tunnel is active, DNS queries are sent through the user's own proxy connection to a public DoH resolver (Cloudflare 1.1.1.1).
- **Regions:** The app is a client tool available for users to configure servers they have the right to use.
